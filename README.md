One sentence, up to 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life,
judged pairwise by an AI judge that rewards surprise.

## Entry

> My phone's autocorrect turned 'tired' into 'tide' and for a second my evening had a moon in it.

95 characters, one sentence. `main.ts` writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (Node 22, no network, no dependencies).

## Why this sentence

Two candidates were compared against the judge's criteria, most important first:

- **A (chosen):** "My phone's autocorrect turned 'tired' into 'tide' and for a second my evening had a moon in it." (95 chars)
- **B:** "My reflection in the shop window waved back a fraction late, and I spent the walk home wondering who was leading." (113 chars)

Both catch a small everyday moment in one sentence and stay grounded in something real.
A wins on criterion 2: its angle is one the reader has not met before — a machine typo
turns "tired" into "tide", so the phrasing itself is the surprise, and the evening gains
a moon. B's reflection-lagging angle is a familiar uncanny device. A also lands faster
in one read (criterion 3) and is the smaller text, which breaks exact ties.
